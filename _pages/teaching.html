Student supervision

I supervise master's theses in business engineering, business information systems engineering, information management, and artificial intelligence. Topics range from process mining to natural language processing, sometimes in collaboration with external partners.

I have co-supervised PhD candidate Yongbo Yu since 2023 and served as a PhD jury member for Brecht Wuyts from 2022 to 2026.
