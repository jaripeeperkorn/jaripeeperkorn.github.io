Processes change over time: some activities become more frequent, while others disappear. Process model forecasting predicts these changes across the whole process, helping us anticipate its future structure and behaviour.

## Time Series Foundation Models for Process Model Forecasting

*CAiSE 2026.*

We study whether models pretrained on large collections of time series can forecast changes in process behaviour. We compare using these models directly with adapting them to process data.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-28110-4_5) · [Code](https://github.com/YongboYu/pmf-tsfm)

## A benchmarking study on process model forecasting: univariate vs. multivariate approaches

*Process Science, 2025.*

We compare methods that forecast activity-to-activity transitions separately with methods that model several transitions together. The benchmark covers statistical, machine-learning, and deep-learning approaches on real event logs.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44311-025-00031-7) · [Code and data](https://github.com/YongboYu/PMF-Benchmark)

## Multivariate Approaches for Process Model Forecasting

*ML4PM at ICPM 2024 · Proceedings published in 2025 · Best Paper Award.*

This work investigates forecasting several process transitions jointly to capture relationships between them. It compares machine-learning and deep-learning methods across different real processes.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-82225-4_21)
