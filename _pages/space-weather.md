Space-weather research studies how activity from the Sun affects Earth's environment. My earlier work explored machine learning for anticipating geomagnetic storms.

## Forecasting geomagnetic storms using long short-term memory neural networks

*Poster, European Space Weather Week (ESWW), 2019 · With Romain Dupuis and Giovanni Lapenta.*

This work investigates neural networks that learn from sequences of observations to forecast geomagnetic storms. It formed part of my master's research in astronomy and astrophysics.
