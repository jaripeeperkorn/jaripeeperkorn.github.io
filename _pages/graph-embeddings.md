Graph embeddings turn relationships in process data into numerical representations that machine-learning models can use. These works study how to include process knowledge in those representations and apply them to predictive process monitoring.

## Enhancing Remaining Time Prediction in Business Processes through Graph Embedding

*HICSS 2025.*

We use graph embeddings to represent relationships between process activities for remaining-time prediction. These representations can be used with different prediction algorithms and are evaluated on real event logs.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.24251/HICSS.2025.139)

## Vector Representation for Business Process: Graph Embedding for Domain Knowledge Integration

*ICMLA 2023.*

We investigate graph embeddings as a way to represent business-process data and incorporate domain knowledge.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICMLA58977.2023.00087)
