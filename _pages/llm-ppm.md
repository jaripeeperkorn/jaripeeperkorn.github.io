Large language models learn patterns in sequences. We study how to adapt them to sequences of business events, so they can predict what happens next while keeping training costs manageable.

## Domain Adaptation of LLMs for Process Data

*ML4PM / ICPM 2025 · Process Mining Workshops (2027).*

We adapt pretrained language models directly to event sequences and evaluate both individual and combined prediction tasks. The approach updates only a small part of the model, reducing the number of parameters that need to be trained.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-18408-5_25) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03161)

## Parameter-Efficient and Quantized LLM Fine-Tuning for Predictive Process Monitoring

*Journal submission; preprint (2026).*

We study how limited model updates and lower-precision computations balance prediction quality, memory use, and training time. The comparison covers predicting the next activity, the next resource, and the remaining time of a case.

[Preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7294110)
