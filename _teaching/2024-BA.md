I am the principal teacher of Business Analytics, a 6 ECTS course in KU Leuven's Advanced Program on Artificial Intelligence in Business and Industry (Advanced Master and Postgraduate Certificate).

**Academic years:** 2023–2024, 2024–2025, and 2025–2026.

The course covers business applications of machine learning, cost-sensitive analytics, fraud analytics, business process management, causal machine learning, and managerial aspects of analytics.
